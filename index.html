ap’s Blurbs

About me: Hi!! I’m ap. I study the genetics of common bean (Phaseolus vulgaris): calling variants from sequencing data, finding the genes behind traits with GWAS, and figuring out how the same line behaves in different field environments.

Who I’d like to meet: Breeders, statisticians, bioinformaticians, and anyone who has ever written “final_v2_REAL_final.R”.
